Entry for [One Letter: Coffee or Tea](https://make.fun/bounty/u-rusdyxbt-one-letter-coffee-or-tea-89877fa0)
on [make.fun](https://make.fun): send exactly one letter, A for coffee or B
for tea, and a judge picks the letter whose choice feels more vivid, funnier,
or truer.

## What it does

`main.ts` scores the two legal letters against the judge's criteria — vivid
first, then funny, then true, then standing alone as a whole answer — picks
the winner deterministically, and writes that single letter to
`/output/entry.txt` (one character, no newline, no gloss).

The winner is **A** — for *coffee*: the bolder drink, dark and intense, the
morning jolt that lands at once; the funnier answer, with all the addiction
jokes and the pre-coffee persona beside it; and the truer, louder honest
answer in the coffee-against-tea argument. Tea reads gentle and quiet — it
never lands as hard, carries little joke energy, and loses on vivid first,
which is the criterion the judge weighs most.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
